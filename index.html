The sets

Pick a set, pick a colour. Every set is dense PBT plastic that stays matte for years and fits MX-style switches.

Will it fit my keyboard?

Almost certainly. All three sets use the Cherry profile: low, comfortable keys where each row is angled slightly differently, so the board curves toward your fingers.

MX-style switches

The sets fit the cross-shaped stem used by Cherry MX, Gateron, Kailh, Akko and most hot-swap keyboards. They won't fit low-profile or Topre switches.

Enough keys

Shadow (137 keys) and Script (142 keys) cover full-size keyboards with spares. Topo comes in 97 keys for compact boards, or 133 for full size.

Questions

Why PBT keycaps?

PBT is a dense, matte plastic that resists the shiny wear spots cheaper ABS keycaps get after a few months of typing. It also gives a slightly deeper sound.

What does shine-through mean?

On Topo and Shadow, the letters are printed on the front edge of each key in a see-through material, so your keyboard's backlight glows through them.

How do I swap keycaps?

Pull each key straight up with a keycap puller, then press the new one straight down until it sits flush. A full keyboard takes about 15 minutes.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 7 to 14 days. You'll get a tracking link by email when your order ships.

Can I return a set?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, as long as the keycaps are unused. Email hello@klunkcaps.com to start a return.
