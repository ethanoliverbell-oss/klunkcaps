Will these fit my keyboard?

If your switches have a "+" shaped stem (Cherry MX, Gateron, Kailh, Akko and most hot-swap boards), yes. Phantom and Script cover full-size boards with spares. Topo comes in 97 keys for compact boards or 133 for full size.

What does shine-through mean?

On Topo and Phantom the letters are see-through, so your keyboard's backlight glows through them. Script's letters are dyed into the plastic, so they never wear off but don't light up.

How do I swap keycaps?

Pull each key straight up with a keycap puller, then press the new one straight down until it sits flush. A full board takes about 15 minutes.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship free to every EU country and the US. A few remote areas, like the Canary Islands and French overseas regions, aren't covered yet. Your order ships from our partner warehouses, mostly in China.

Are these safe for kids?

Keycaps are small parts and can be a choking hazard, so keep them away from children under 3. Full details are on our product safety page.
