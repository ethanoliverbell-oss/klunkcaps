Product safety

This information is provided under the EU General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988.

Warnings

  • Small parts. Choking hazard. Not suitable for children under 3 years. Keep keycaps and the keycap puller away from small children.
  • Not a toy. Do not put keycaps in your mouth.
  • Fit only onto keyboards with MX-style ("+") stems. Remove keycaps by pulling straight up with the supplied puller.
  • Dispose of packaging in household recycling. Keycaps are PBT plastic.
